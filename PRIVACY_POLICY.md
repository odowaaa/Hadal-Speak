# Privacy Policy for Hadal English

**Effective Date:** January 30, 2025
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

## Introduction

Hadal English ("we," "our," or "us") is a language-learning app for Somali speakers
learning English. This policy explains what data the app handles and how, in plain
language. The short version: **Hadal English does not have a server or an account
system. Everything the app remembers about you stays on your device.**

## Information the App Stores

Hadal English stores the following only in your device's local browser/app storage
(`localStorage`), never on a remote server:

- **Learning progress:** which lessons you've completed and your quiz scores
- **Preferences:** language (English/Somali), dark mode, notification and audio
  settings, and lesson-reminder settings

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and we have no way to access
it remotely. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data deletes it
permanently. You can also erase it at any time from **Profile → Reset Progress**.

## Information the App Does Not Collect

Hadal English does **not**:
- require you to create an account or provide a name, email, or password
- collect analytics, usage tracking, or advertising identifiers
- use cookies or any third-party tracking scripts
- transmit your learning progress, quiz scores, or settings to any server
- record or upload audio — pronunciation practice uses your device's built-in
  speech recognition and text-to-speech, processed entirely on-device by your
  operating system/browser, never sent to us

## Microphone and Speech Features

The pronunciation-practice feature uses your browser or device's built-in Web
Speech APIs to convert speech to text and text to speech. Audio is processed by
your device/browser (and, depending on your device settings, its manufacturer's
on-device or cloud speech service) — Hadal English itself never receives, stores,
or transmits your voice recordings.

## Third-Party Services

- **Google Play Services / app store:** used only for distributing and installing
  the app; governed by Google's own privacy policy.
- No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs are included in the app.

## Children's Privacy

Hadal English is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone, including children. Since the app has no
accounts or data collection, there is no personal data to request the deletion of
beyond what is described above (which you can already clear yourself at any time).

## Your Choices

- **Reset your data:** Profile → Reset Progress clears all locally stored progress.
- **Change language/appearance:** Profile → Settings.
- **Uninstall the app** or clear the site's storage in your browser to remove all
  locally stored data immediately.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds optional
cloud sync — we will update the "Last Updated" date above and describe the change
here before it takes effect.
